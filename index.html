Submit an idea

Have an idea for Plymouth? The commission reviews every submission. Promising ideas are developed and brought to the City Council. If an idea isn't a good fit, you'll get an honest answer.

We ask for your address only to confirm you live in Plymouth. Submissions may be public records.

Volunteer

Join our volunteer list and we'll reach out when the City needs extra hands. Volunteers help with:

  • Cleanup after storms in parks, sidewalks, and public areas
  • Setting up, taking down, and cleaning up after events
  • Litter pickup, weeding, planting, and watering
  • Holiday decorating and painting over minor graffiti
  • Handing out flyers and helping with surveys

Volunteers only do simple, safe tasks. Everyone signs in and signs a waiver at each event.

About the commission

The Civic Engagement Commission is a five-member advisory body. Each City Council member appoints one commissioner. The commission makes recommendations; all decisions stay with the City Council.
