Have a say in what happens in Plymouth

Not everyone can make it to a 6:30 meeting. The Civic Engagement Commission is a five-member advisory group that helps the City hear from more residents. We collect ideas, run surveys, and organize volunteers, then bring what we learn to the City Council.

The commission is advisory only. All decisions stay with the City Council.

Submit an idea

Every idea gets read. Good ones are worked into a plan and brought to the Council. If an idea isn't a fit, we'll tell you why.

We ask for your address only to confirm you live in Plymouth. Please note that submissions may be public records.

Volunteer

Public Works has a small crew covering a lot of ground. Volunteers help with the small jobs so staff can focus on the bigger ones:

  • Cleanup after storms in parks, sidewalks, and public areas
  • Event setup, takedown, and cleanup
  • Litter pickup, weeding, planting, and watering
  • Holiday decorating and painting over minor graffiti
  • Handing out flyers and helping with surveys

Volunteers only do simple, safe tasks and sign in at each event. Join the volunteer list (opens in a new tab)
