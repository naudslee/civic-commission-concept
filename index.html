Have a say in what happens in Plymouth

The Civic Engagement Commission is a five-member advisory group that helps the City hear from more residents. We collect ideas, run surveys, and organize volunteers, then bring what we learn to the City Council.

The commission is advisory only. All decisions stay with the City Council.

Volunteer

Looking for volunteer opportunities? Our volunteer group assists with:

  • Cleanup after storms in parks, sidewalks, and public areas
  • Event setup, takedown, and cleanup
  • Litter pickup, weeding, planting, and watering
  • Holiday decorating and painting over minor graffiti
  • Handing out flyers and helping with surveys

Volunteers only do simple, safe tasks and sign in at each event. Join the volunteer list (opens in a new tab)
